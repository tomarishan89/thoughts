## 2. Etymological and Classical Textual Alignment

The word *Dharma* derives from the Sanskrit verbal root:
$$\sqrt{\text{धृ}} \quad (\textit{dhṛ}) \quad \longrightarrow \quad \textit{dhāraṇapoṣaṇayoḥ} \quad (\text{“to hold, sustain, support, maintain structural integrity”})$$

In the *Mahabharata* (Karna Parva, 69.58), this structural sustenance is stated explicitly:
$$\text{\textit{“Dhāraṇāt dharmam ityāhuḥ, dharmo dhārayate prajāḥ”}}$$
*(“Dharma is so named because it sustains; Dharma maintains the structured order of entities.”)*

Under this physical definition, an entity’s *Svadharma* is not an arbitrary cultural commandment, but the **mathematically constrained operational Lie algebra ($D_{\mathfrak{Im}}$)** that generates internal Cauchy stress to sustain boundary coherence against environmental challenge tractions ($\mathbf{C}$).
