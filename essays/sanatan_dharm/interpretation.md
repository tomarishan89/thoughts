## 1. Universal Sanatan Dharm ($\mathcal{D}_T$)

Let $I(t)$ be the index set of all extant forms of existence at time $t$ across the universal spacetime manifold $\mathcal{M} = T \times \mathbb{R}^n$. 

The Universal Matrix of Structural Persistence Laws, designated as **Sanatan Dharm** ($\mathcal{D}_T$), is the uncreated, eternal set-theoretic union of all localized Svadharma operator sets ($D_{\mathfrak{Im}}^i(t)$) across all spacetime coordinates $T \times I$:

$$\mathcal{D}_T \equiv \bigcup_{t \in T} \bigcup_{i \in I(t)} D_{\mathfrak{Im}}^i(t)$$

For any active temporal domain $T_{\text{active}} \subseteq T$ where at least one form of existence persists ($\exists E^i(t) \neq \emptyset$), the master matrix $\mathcal{D}_T$ is strictly non-empty:

$$\Big( \forall t \in T_{\text{active}}, \; \exists E^i(t) \neq \emptyset \Big) \implies \mathcal{D}_T \neq \emptyset \quad \text{and} \quad \lim_{|T_{\text{active}}| \to \infty} \mu(\mathcal{D}_T) = \infty$$

Sanatan Dharm represents the totality of all physical, biological, cognitive, and structural principles that permit any bounded entity to maintain topological enclosure against entropic dissolution across all epochs of the universe.

## 2. Svadharma Inclusion Principle

For any individual form of existence $E^i(t)$, its localized operational rule set $D_{\mathfrak{Im}}^i(t)$ (**Svadharma**) is a contextual, bounded proper subset of the universal matrix $\mathcal{D}_T$:

$$D_{\mathfrak{Im}}^i(t) = \bigcup_{k \in K(t)} \mathcal{O}_k(t) \quad \subset \quad \mathcal{D}_T, \quad \text{where } \mu(D_{\mathfrak{Im}}^i(t)) \ll \mu(\mathcal{D}_T)$$

### Etymological and Classical Textual Alignment

The term *Dharma* derives from the Sanskrit root:

$$\sqrt{\text{धृ}} \quad (\textit{dhṛ}) \quad \longrightarrow \quad \textit{dhāraṇapoṣaṇayoḥ} \quad (\text{“to hold, sustain, support, maintain structural integrity”})$$

In classical literature, this structural sustenance is stated explicitly:

$$\text{\textit{“Dhāraṇāt dharmam ityāhuḥ, dharmo dhārayate prajāḥ”}}$$
*(“Dharma is so named because it sustains; Dharma maintains the structured order of entities.”)*

Under this definition, an entity’s *Svadharma* is not a set of arbitrary cultural or moral commandments, but the specific, mathematically constrained operational operators that sustain its boundary coherence against environmental stress.

1. **Universal Matrix Definition:** Sanatan Dharm ($\mathcal{D}_T$) is the universal union of all operational principles across all entities and all epochs: $\mathcal{D}_T = \bigcup_{t} \bigcup_{i} D_{\mathfrak{Im}}^i(t)$.
2. **Localized Entity Definition:** An individual entity $E^i(t)$ is a finite locus with bounded measure ($\mu(E^i(t)) < \infty$).
3. **The Paradox:** The common assertion *"I follow / practice Sanatan Dharm"* commits a category error equivalent to a single hydrogen atom declaring *"I embody the total set of all quantum electrodynamic, nuclear, and cosmic gravitational equations."*

An individual entity cannot "follow" or "be" Sanatan Dharm. An entity merely instantiates its localized, contextual proper subset:

$$D_{\mathfrak{Im}}^i(t) \subset \mathcal{D}_T$$
