## Milestone Epistemic Classification Taxonomy

To enforce strict mathematical rigor and maintain transparent epistemic boundaries for paper submission, all theoretical milestones and claims are classified across three rigorous categories:

- **Type (a) — Original Derivation:** A novel mathematical derivation, theorem, or quantitative prediction originating uniquely within this framework (e.g., the $\Omega_\Lambda = 2/3$ membrane tension theorem, dynamic inflow boundary renormalization $\delta\Omega_m = -4G\dot{M}/(3c^3)$, scalar amplitude $A_s$ from Parker creation with GUT loop suppression).
- **Type (b) — Standard Application:** A mathematically rigorous, standard application of existing published physics results to the framework's specific context (e.g., FLRW minisuperspace Wheeler-DeWitt quantization following DeWitt 1967, ECSK spin-torsion bounce dynamics following Popławski 2010, Schwarzschild-Hubble horizon radius equality $R_s \equiv R_H$ following Dicke 1957 / Melia 2012).
- **Type (c) — Domain-Extrapolated / Unverified Applicability:** Application of a standard physical or mathematical tool to an exotic or non-standard domain where empirical or microphysical validity remains unverified or heuristic (e.g., Atiyah-Patodi-Singer index theorem on classical lipid bilayer pore transitions, Novikov-Shubin invariants on finite syncytial cell networks, Kähler $\nabla J = 0$ on non-equilibrium metabolic manifolds).

> **Paper Submission Scope Policy:** The Tier 1 journal submission focuses exclusively on Type (a) original derivations and Type (b) standard physical applications within relativistic cosmology, gravitational physics, and continuum mechanics. All Type (c) biophysical and cognitive extrapolations are strictly segregated into Tier 2–4, logged for meta-theoretical completeness, and deferred from the paper submission scope.
