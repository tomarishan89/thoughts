## 2. Interior-Exterior Time: The Micro-Black-Hole Thought Experiment

> Exterior Schwarzschild time $t_{\text{ext}} \to \infty$ freezes at $r = R_s$, while interior Lemaitre proper time $\tau$ across the collapse is finite ( $\sim 10^{-4}$ s for $30 M_\odot$ ). 2. **The interior proper time is NOT determined by the exterior observer's measurement of the BH lifetime.** The interior has its own Hubble parameter $H_{\text{interior}}$, its own scale factor $a(t)$, its own cosmological history.

### 2.3 The Micro Black Hole Case (LHC Hypothetical)

Hypothetical micro black holes at the LHC would have mass $M \sim 1\text{ TeV}/c^2 \sim 1.8 \times 10^{-24}\text{ kg}$.

**Exterior quantities (our frame):**

- Schwarzschild radius: $R_s = 2GM/c^2 \sim 2.7 \times 10^{-51}\text{ m}$ (far sub-Planckian — this is why most physicists believe such objects cannot form classically)
- Hawking evaporation time: $t_{\text{evap}} = \frac{5120\pi G^2 M^3}{\hbar c^4} \sim 10^{-88}\text{ s}$ (not picoseconds — incomprehensibly shorter)

**Interior quantities (if classical GR held):**

- Maximum interior proper time from horizon to singularity (Schwarzschild): $\tau_{\text{max}} = \pi GM/c^3 \sim 10^{-62}\text{ s}$
- This is the longest any geodesic inside the horizon can last before hitting the classical singularity.

So in standard Schwarzschild GR: **No.** The interior does not have trillions of years. It has $10^{-62}$ seconds. Less than the exterior, not more.
