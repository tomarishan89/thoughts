$$E(t+dt) = \hat{U}\!\left[E(t),\; \{E_k^{\mathfrak{Im}}(t)\}_{k=1}^{N}\right]$$

**The next Iteration is different from the previous Iteration in both imaginary and physical space** precisely because the imaginary sub-Egos generated during the previous Iteration are now present as new inputs to the next one. The imaginary space does not reset between Iterations — it accumulates and compounds.

## The Dependency Structure

There is a strict hierarchy:

$$\text{Imaginary sub-Ego } E_k^{\mathfrak{Im}} \quad \xrightarrow{\text{depends on}} \quad \text{Host Real Ego } \partial E_{\mathbb{R}}$$

If the host's real Ego collapses (death, lysis), all imaginary sub-Egos collapse instantly — they have no independent metabolic substrate. They are **parasitic forms of existence** on the host's Landauer budget.

However, some imaginary sub-Egos can **escape into shared imaginary space** — when the host expresses them into the world (writing a book, building an institution), the imaginary Ego is written into the shared Ledger $\mathcal{F}_{\text{ledger}}^{\text{external}}$ of other entities, and gains a degree of independence from the original host. This is cultural transmission — the imaginary Ego of a long-dead person continues to exist in the imaginary spaces of those who read their work.
